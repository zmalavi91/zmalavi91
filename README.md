## About Me

I am an early-career scholar and educator in Political Science and International Relations, specializing in human rights governance, foreign policy analysis, geopolitics, major-power competition, Middle East and Gulf politics, and the relationship between artificial intelligence and global order.

I currently serve as Guest Faculty in Political Science at Women’s College, Aligarh Muslim University. I teach undergraduate courses within the National Education Policy 2020 and Four-Year Undergraduate Programme framework.

My doctoral research examined the role of the United States and the European Union in shaping human-rights norms, international frameworks, and geopolitical dynamics after the Cold War.
